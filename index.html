How it works

Three simple steps for the school demonstration.

1. Register: Student details and item information are entered.

2. Store: Staff assigns an available compartment.

3. Claim: Staff scans or enters the unique code, checks the record, then releases the item.

The QR code contains a transaction ID only. Staff should confirm the student's details before releasing belongings.
